# Decision log

This log captures choices that intentionally constrain the portfolio explorer. Every reversal must review its trigger and migration impact first.

Reversibility levels: **Easy** is localized, **Moderate** changes shared types or several components, and **Hard** affects identity, persisted data, or foundational architecture.

## Accepted decisions

| ID | Status | Context | Alternatives | Decision | Rationale | Reversibility | Reversal trigger | Migration notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-001 | Accepted | The app needs a realistic full-stack boundary for future provider credentials. | Vite-only React, Next.js without Vinext, or another framework. | Use Next.js/Vinext with TypeScript. | Keeps the interview app close to a deployable server/client shape without introducing live secrets now. | **Hard later** | Reconsider only before substantial route and component code exists. | A framework change rewrites UI, server boundaries, routing, and build configuration. |
| D-002 | Accepted | Live indexers add setup, credential, rate-limit, and precision risk. | Build directly against Zerion/Zapper, or make the UI fixture-specific. | Use a neutral `PortfolioProvider` with deterministic fixtures. | Lets core aggregation and UX be tested quickly while preserving an adapter boundary. | **Easy** | A live provider is selected. | Substitute the provider implementation and preserve normalized contracts. |
| D-003 | Accepted | Multi-chain support is required, but non-EVM chains expand address and token semantics. | EVM plus Solana in core, or single-chain only. | Ship Ethereum, Base, and Polygon with namespaced `eip155:*` IDs. | Covers the prompt's multi-network requirement while leaving room for non-EVM namespaces later. | **Moderate** | A non-EVM family becomes valuable enough to justify new adapter semantics. | Add another namespace and adapter; do not reinterpret existing `eip155:*` IDs. |
| D-004 | Accepted | Symbols collide and provider IDs are not portable across providers. | Symbol-only IDs, provider IDs, or chain/contract IDs only. | Separate chain/contract instance IDs from canonical asset IDs. | Instance identity protects correctness; canonical identity enables intentional grouping. | **Hard** | The entire identity policy is replaced. | Version IDs and migrate caches, URLs, watch lists, and any persisted user preferences. |
| D-005 | Accepted | Equivalence can come from a maintained registry or an external token catalog. | Merge by symbol, use provider equivalence blindly, or integrate a live catalog now. | Use a small verified registry; never merge by symbol. | The core can prove USDC merging while avoiding accidental merges for unknown contracts. | **Easy** | Static coverage becomes unmanageable. | Replace the lookup implementation while preserving canonical IDs. |
| D-006 | Accepted | Zerion and Zapper may report the same target balances. | Sum every provider result, choose one provider globally, or merge fields by precedence. | One provider is authoritative for quantities per wallet/network target; another may enrich or act as fallback. | Prevents double counting and keeps failures attributable per target. | **Moderate/Hard** | A proven need exists to merge provider fields for the same target. | Define deduplication keys and field precedence first; never sum duplicate provider balances. |
| D-007 | Accepted | Separate grouping models would duplicate totals, sorting, and search behavior. | Separate token, network, and wallet aggregation code paths. | Derive every grouping from one normalized positions collection. | Switching grouping modes stays a selector concern rather than a refactor. | **Moderate** | New position kinds cannot be represented safely. | Extend with discriminated position kinds instead of adding parallel models. |
| D-008 | Accepted | Search can filter source positions or already-built group labels. | Search after aggregation. | Search before aggregation. | Visible totals and percentages match the filtered subset, e.g. searching "Base" produces Base-only totals. | **Easy** | Product research favors group-label search instead. | Reverse selector order; source data and provider contracts stay unchanged. |
| D-009 | Accepted | Token, network, or wallet grouping could be the initial lens. | Network-first or wallet-first. | Default to token grouping. | Matches common portfolio explorer behavior and foregrounds normalized assets. | **Easy** | Usage favors another default. | Change the default UI state. |
| D-010 | Accepted | Multi-currency valuation expands pricing scope. | Currency selector in core, or no valuation. | Core valuation is USD; unpriced assets stay visible and are excluded from totals. | Keeps totals understandable while not hiding positions with missing prices. | **Moderate** | Multi-fiat becomes required. | Make centralized pricing and formatting currency-aware. |
| D-011 | Accepted | Wallet entry and wallet connection compete with aggregation work. | Injected wallet connection in core, or manual address form. | Use seeded wallet/network targets in core. | Lets the interview demo focus on aggregation, normalization, and failure behavior. | **Easy** | Core acceptance passes and wallet connection becomes the next priority. | Convert connected addresses and selected networks into `WalletTarget` records. |
| D-012 | Accepted | Aggregate rows are enough for core, but composition helps users trust totals. | No expansion, separate details page, or always-visible children. | Add expandable child positions after core acceptance. | Gives transparent provenance without changing aggregate math. | **Easy** | Interaction threatens core reliability or demo clarity. | Remove expansion UI and keep aggregate rows, counts, and selectors. |
| D-013 | Accepted | Fixture pagination is not needed, but live indexers return paged data. | Ignore pagination until live integration, or add UI pagination now. | Keep `BalancePage.nextCursor` in the provider contract; fixtures return `null`. | The provider boundary is ready for cursor traversal without adding stretch UI. | **Easy** | A live provider adapter is implemented. | Follow all cursors before marking a target complete. |
| D-014 | Accepted | The prompt asks to document decisions and reversal difficulty. | Keep decisions in chat only. | Maintain `docs/decisions.md` and link it from the README. | Preserves the interview reasoning and makes future reversals explicit. | **Easy** | The project moves to another ADR format. | Preserve ID, status, rationale, reversibility, trigger, and migration fields. |
| D-015 | Accepted | Fixture quantities are fast, but live balances are often integer strings plus decimals. | Use a decimal library now, or keep numbers temporarily. | Use JavaScript numbers for fixtures only. | Sufficient for deterministic demo values and simpler tests. | **Easy now, harder after live integration** | Before accepting real provider balances. | Perform a precision review and move quantities/values to a decimal-safe representation if needed. |
| D-016 | Accepted | Quantity and USD valuation may come from different systems. | Keep one generic provider field, or split balance and price provenance. | Track balance provenance and price provenance separately on each normalized position. | Users need to know whether an incomplete value came from a failed balance request or a missing price source. | **Moderate** | A richer valuation service needs confidence, quote type, or multiple price candidates. | Extend `source` fields without changing asset identity or grouping selectors. |

## Deferred decisions

| ID | Status | Context | Alternatives | Decision | Rationale | Reversibility | Reversal trigger | Migration notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D-017 | Deferred | Live providers can supply balances, metadata, history, and transactions. | Zerion first, Zapper first, direct RPC, or multiple providers at once. | Zerion is the first planned live adapter; Zapper is an alternative or fallback. | Zerion/Zapper both help replace mocked calls, but provider merging requires care. | **Easy/Moderate** | Coverage, pricing, latency, or reliability makes the first provider unsuitable. | Add one adapter through `PortfolioProvider`; do not blindly sum equivalent provider outputs. |
| D-018 | Deferred | Watch lists need stable asset identity beyond current holdings. | Symbol keys, instance keys, or canonical keys. | Future watch lists should use versioned canonical asset IDs. | Users usually watch assets, not specific bridge instances, but persisted IDs are expensive to change. | **Hard once persisted** | Canonical policy changes before watch lists ship. | Version local storage and provide migrations before persistence ships. |
| D-019 | Deferred | Transaction history and historical portfolio value are stretch goals. | Overload `NormalizedPosition`, or create dedicated models. | Use dedicated transaction and history models later. | Current holdings should stay simple and not become an all-purpose ledger type. | **Moderate** | Stretch work begins. | Add separate provider contracts and views; keep current holdings selectors unchanged. |

## Implementation reconciliation

- Core milestones 0-4 are implemented with deterministic fixtures, scenario URLs, and regression tests.
- Optional group composition is implemented as expandable aggregate rows.
- No live provider, wallet connection, history chart, transaction history, pagination UI, or watch-list persistence is present in the core.
- The only precision shortcut is fixture-only numeric quantities, tracked in D-015.
